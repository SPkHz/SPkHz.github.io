## Overview
This lab studies a 2N3904 common-emitter amplifier with emitter degeneration, focusing on (1) the DC operating point, (2) the midband small-signal gain, (3) the frequency response (Bode plot), and (4) the maximum input/output swing before visible distortion.

## Circuit under test
Component values (from the handout):

- $V_{CC}=12\,\text{V}$
- $R_C=909\,\Omega$, $R_{E1}=200\,\Omega$, $R_{E2}=392\,\Omega$
- $R_{B1}=27\,\text{k}\Omega$, $R_{B2}=12\,\text{k}\Omega$
- $R_{sig}=1\,\text{k}\Omega$, $R_L=10\,\text{k}\Omega$
- $C_{B1}=10\,\mu\text{F}$, $C_{B2}=10\,\mu\text{F}$, $C_{B3}=100\,\mu\text{F}$

## Measurement plan
Following the lab procedure, the measurements include:

1. DC operating point: measure $V_B$, $V_C$, $V_E$ and determine $I_C$.
2. Gain vs. frequency: measure $G_v(f)=\tilde{V}_o(f)/\tilde{V}_{sig}(f)$ from 10 Hz to 10 MHz.
3. Midband gain at 10 kHz: measure $G_{v(\text{mid})}=v_o/v_{sig}$ at $f=10\,\text{kHz}$.
4. Linearity: increase input until output is visibly distorted and record $V_{psig(max)}$ and $V_{po(max)}$.

### Notes on $I_C$
The measured collector current was obtained from the measured $V_C$ using
$$(V_{CC}-V_C)=I_C\,R_C,$$
which gives $I_C=4.7116\,\text{mA}$ for $V_{CC}=12\,\text{V}$, $V_C=7.7172\,\text{V}$, and $R_C=909\,\Omega$.

## Frequency response (Bode plot)
The gain magnitude is flat through the midband and rolls off at low and high frequency, consistent with the coupling/bypass capacitors and the transistor’s high-frequency limits.

## Discussion (what changed between theory, simulation, and hardware)
- The DC bias point matched closely: $V_C$, $V_E$, $V_B$, and $I_C$ were all within ~3% of calculated values.
- The midband gain in **V/V** differed more (13.7% vs calculated), but the **dB** value was within 0.68 dB, indicating the same general gain level but with expected parameter variation.
- The measured maximum input and output swings were within ~9% of calculated values.
