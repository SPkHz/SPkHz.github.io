Measuring the Non-Ideal Characteristics of an Operational Amplifier (LM741 Op-Amp)

Non-ideal op-amp characteristics measured in LTspice—offset voltage, input bias currents, slew rate, and gain-bandwidth product.

This lab explores non-ideal characteristics of the LM741 operational amplifier using a set of DC test circuits and frequency/time-domain measurements:

  • DC imperfections: input offset voltage (V{OS}) and input bias currents (I{BN}), (I_{BP})
  • Dynamic limits: slew rate (large-signal step response)
  • Small-signal behavior: Bode magnitude, (f{H}) (−3 dB frequency), and (f{t}) (unity-gain / gain-bandwidth)

Tools: LTspice (operating point, transient, AC analysis) + MATLAB (solving for (V{OS}), (I{BN}), (I_{BP})).

Setup

  • Op-amp: LM741
  • Supplies: (\pm 12\,\text{V})
  • Resistors: 470 Ω, 4.7 kΩ, 1 MΩ

Non-ideal model used for DC analysis

The DC imperfection model adds:

  • (V_{OS}): a small differential input offset voltage
  • (I{BN}), (I{BP}): bias currents flowing into the (−) and (+) input terminals

Part A — DC imperfections (operating point)

Four DC test circuits were simulated using LTspice (.op). By changing the external resistances, the output offset changes in a way that allows solving for (V{OS}), (I{BN}), and (I_{BP}) (using KCL at the input nodes).

Part B — Slew rate (large-signal step response)

A 1 V square wave was applied and the frequency was increased until the output became trapezoidal, indicating the output slope is limited by the amplifier’s slew rate.

Part C — Sine-wave distortion from slew-rate limiting

At low frequency (1 kHz), the output remains sinusoidal. At high frequency (100 kHz), the output becomes more triangular, consistent with a slew-rate limit.

Part D — Bode plot, (f_H), and gain-bandwidth

An AC sweep from 100 Hz to 10 MHz was used to measure (A_v(f)), then:

  • (f_H) was found at the −3 dB point from the low-frequency gain
  • (f_t) was found at (approximately) 0 dB gain
  • (\text{GBP} \approx A_v(0) \cdot f_H)
