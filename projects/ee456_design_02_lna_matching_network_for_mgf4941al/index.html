EE-456 Microwave Active Circuits — Design Project 02

15 GHz low-noise amplifier matching network design (MGF4941AL). Gain/noise/VSWR trade-space + ADS/MATLAB cross-verification.

This project (EE-456: RF & mm-Wave Active Circuits) designs a **15 GHz low-noise amplifier (LNA) matching network** for the **MGF4941AL InGaAs HEMT** at **VDS = 2 V** and **IDS = 10 mA**. Unlike Project 1 (pure max-gain), the goal here is a **practical compromise** that meets **gain + noise + matching** specifications simultaneously, while validating results in **both MATLAB and Keysight ADS**. **Toolchain:** MATLAB + Keysight ADS (ideal transmission-line matching + Touchstone verification) **Center frequency:** f0 = 15 GHz (swept ~14–16 GHz) --- ## Design Specs (Course Targets @ f0) - **Transducer gain:** \(G_T \ge 12\ \text{dB}\) - **Noise figure:** \(NF \le 0.6\ \text{dB}\) - **Matching constraint:** \(VSWR \le 1.5\) (input and output networks) --- ## Strategy: MAG vs Fmin vs “Meets Everything” (Design 2) The design begins with device S-parameters and noise parameters at **15 GHz**, then uses Smith-chart geometry to pick a termination that satisfies the constraints. - **MAG design:** maximizes gain, but noise figure is too high (fails NF spec). - **Fmin design:** hits minimum noise figure, but gain falls short (fails gain spec). - **Design 2 (final):** selects \(\Gamma_S\) and \(\Gamma_L\) from the feasible region defined by **noise figure circles**, **available gain circles**, and **stability constraints**, then realizes the terminations with **single-stub transmission-line networks**. --- ## Network Layout (Final)
The final solution is a compact single-stub IMN + single-stub OMN. The chosen \(\Gamma_S\) comes from the feasible intersection of the gain/noise/stability constraints.
--- ## Final Design Point (15 GHz) | Parameter | Value | |---|---:| | \(\Gamma_S\) | \(0.4256 \angle -136.0227^\circ\) | | \(\Gamma_L\) | \(0.2904 \angle +146.0865^\circ\) | | \(G_T\) | **12.26 dB** | | \(NF\) | **0.580 dB** | | VSWR (IMN) | **1.50** | | VSWR (OMN) | **1.39** | **Electrical lengths (open-stub networks):** - **IMN:** \(\theta_{I1}=43.2481^\circ\) (series TL), \(\theta_{I2}=10.4170^\circ\) (open shunt stub) - **OMN:** \(\theta_{O1}=31.2559^\circ\) (series TL), \(\theta_{O2}=53.5158^\circ\) (open shunt stub) --- ## Verification: ADS vs MATLAB Overlays
ADS and MATLAB were run as independent toolchains (Touchstone export + re-plot). The overlays validate the same operating point and confirm the network behavior around 15 GHz.
--- ## What’s in the repo - **Presentation (PDF/PPTX)** + plot/screenshots used in the slides - **MATLAB code** (design + verification scripts) - **ADS project files** (schematics + simulations) - **Excel tables** (design summary / comparison) - **Touchstone outputs** (`.s2p`) for IMN/OMN/whole network + exported noise data (`.csv`) --- ## Notes / Limitations This is an **ideal transmission-line** matching design used to study the gain/noise/match trade-space cleanly. A layout-ready version would add substrate-backed physical lines (microstrip/CPW), losses, discontinuities, stability across a wider band, and a full bias network + EM-aware validation.
